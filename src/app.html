Late reactions grow

A tailgater sees brake lights about a second late, so they brake harder than the car ahead did. The driver behind them is later still. Each car adds a bit, until someone stops completely.

The jam moves backward

Cars leave the front of the jam while new ones join at the back, so the jam itself drifts upstream at around 15 to 20 km/h. Drivers far behind hit a wall long after the first driver sped up again.

A gap soaks it up

A driver with room ahead can ease off gently and let the gap shrink instead of braking. The wave dies there, and everyone behind them keeps rolling. A few such drivers are enough to calm a whole road.
